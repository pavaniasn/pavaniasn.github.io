Officer on Special Duty (Higher Education)

2021 – 2024
Telangana Social Welfare Residential Educational Institutions Society, Government of Telangana

Headed the academic function for a residential degree college system serving first-generation women learners from marginalised communities — around 18,000 students across 30 colleges, extending to roughly 30,000 across 45 colleges for programmes run jointly with the Tribal Welfare society, and to around 1.5 lakh students through school-level scientific collaborations.

  • Institutional expansion. Led the academic work behind specialised institutions in Pharmacy, Agriculture and Design — the only free residential professional education colleges of their kind in India. Introduced a B.Des. under the mentorship of the National Institute of Fashion Technology, the first regular academic programme in India to run that way, and B.Sc. Bioinformatics, the first such course in Telangana.
  • Foreign languages. Introduced French as a second language at undergraduate level across four campuses, in partnership with the French Embassy and Alliance Française. The pilot cohort of 100 students completed A2 under the CEFR.
  • Access and progression. Led competitive examination preparation across the college system — GRE and IELTS, CAT, CUCET, university entrance and pharmacy entrance — helping hundreds of students from marginalised backgrounds reach premier universities in India and abroad.
  • Outreach. Led a higher education awareness campaign across 451 junior colleges reaching around 19,000 school students, after which course combinations offered in the degree colleges rose by 50 per cent.
  • Research. Convened the institution's first international conference, with 800 delegates and 210 research presentations from four countries, and steered the first funding cycle of its research council across 110 proposals from ten departments.
  • Partnerships. International and industry collaborations spanning universities in the United Kingdom and the United States, national research institutes, and non-profit and corporate partners supporting scholarships, internships and digital access.
  • Policy and curriculum. Member of state higher education council syllabus drafting committees; integrated new STEM and foreign language programmes into undergraduate curricula.
